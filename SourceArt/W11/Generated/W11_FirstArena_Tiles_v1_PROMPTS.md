> images could not guarantee continuous stonework, lighting, or color across the

Shared constraints: straight-down orthographic Chinese xianxia roguelite arena; painterly realistic fantasy; teal-jade flagstones; subdued gold inlay; readable low-contrast combat surface; outer-edge bamboo, lotus water, ruins and mist; no characters, monsters, UI, text, logos, borders, or perspective horizon.

- `W11_FirstArena_NE_v1.png`: northeast tile; right/top outer scenery; left/bottom plain flagstone interior seams.
- `W11_FirstArena_SW_v1.png`: southwest tile; left/bottom outer scenery; right/top plain flagstone interior seams; faint broken formation motif.
- `W11_FirstArena_SE_v1.png`: southeast tile; right/bottom outer scenery; left/top plain flagstone interior seams; shallow rune cracks.
